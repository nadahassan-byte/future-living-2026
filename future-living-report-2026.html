Their view of short-term prices has cooled. Their commitment to Dubai hasn’t.

How do Dubai residents feel about the city, and about property, after a period of regional uncertainty? For the third year running, we asked them. 1,714 residents from 63 nationalities told us how they live, rent, buy and invest.

Confidence

Confidence in Dubai held.

A year of regional uncertainty tested how residents feel about the city. Most came through it surer of Dubai, not less.

Commitment

Commitment runs long.

Residents are staying longer and owning more. The share who plan to stay a decade or more has climbed with every edition of this survey.

Buying intent

Buying plans didn’t follow prices.

Price expectations cooled sharply this year. Tenants’ plans to buy did not move with them.

01 Confidence

Confidence in Dubai held when it was tested

This is the first Future Living survey since a period of regional disruption. For 45% of residents, confidence in Dubai did not merely hold, it rose. Property plans moved more carefully than sentiment did.

What it meansMost residents still believe in Dubai. Some are just taking a little longer to decide when to buy or move.

More residents are putting down roots

Homeownership has risen in every edition of this survey, and the residents who have been here longest are the ones most likely to stay.

What it meansThe longer people live here, the more likely they are to stay. 75% of residents who’ve been here more than ten years plan to stay another decade.

Where does Dubai stand among the world’s property markets?

Most residents already place Dubai among the world’s leading property markets, or expect it to get there within five years. 70% are confident in the city’s long-term prospects.

What it meansMost residents see Dubai as a world-class property market, or one well on its way. We’ve watched it grow since 1986.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The answers below are what residents told us, not a market forecast.

Are Dubai property prices expected to fall in 2026?

Residents expect a calmer market. In our Future Living 2026 survey of 1,714 Dubai residents, 32% expect sale prices to rise in the next 12 months, down from 72% in 2024, while 53% expect a fall, including 27% who expect a drop of more than 10%. These are residents’ views, not a market forecast. 70% remain confident in Dubai’s long-term prospects.

Will my rent go up when I renew in Dubai?

Most tenants don’t expect it to. 34% expect a rent increase at renewal, down from 77% in 2024, and 66% expect their rent to hold or fall. Any increase at renewal must still follow the RERA rental index.

Are tenants still planning to buy property in Dubai?

Yes. 55% of tenants plan to buy within one to three years, the same as in 2025. 67% of them plan to use a mortgage, and long-term residence is the most common reason for buying, given by 71%.

What type of property are Dubai buyers looking for now?

More space. Among tenants planning to buy, townhouse preference has risen to 30%, from 17% in 2024, while apartment preference has fallen to 41%. 23% want four bedrooms or more. Existing owners lean towards ready homes (41%) over off-plan (27%) for their next purchase.

How has regional uncertainty affected confidence in Dubai?

It held. 67% said their confidence in Dubai stayed the same or improved, and 45% said it rose. Property decisions moved more carefully: 36% saw no change to their plans, 31% became more committed or accelerated plans, and 33% slowed, paused or reassessed.

How long do Dubai residents plan to stay?

69% plan to stay for ten years or more, up from 59% in 2025. Among residents who have already lived here for more than ten years, it’s 75%.
